Let’s clear a few things up.

Is Alliances only for CS students?

No. Alliances is for students and faculty from every department. A medical student can find a developer for a health-tech idea, a pharma student can work with someone from medicine, and researchers can find people from completely different fields to work with.

The point is to make it easier to find people you wouldn't normally meet on campus.

But I don't know anyone here.

That's exactly who this is for. You send a request with a short note, and the person who posted looks at your profile and decides. You never have to cold message anyone.

What if nobody replies, or they say no?

You won't be left wondering forever. Every post has a deadline, and once it passes, open requests expire and everyone gets a notification. If someone declines, they can choose a polite reason and add a note if they want to explain.

And if plans change, the person who posted can always reopen the request.

Can strangers message me?

No. Nobody can start a chat with you out of nowhere. A chat opens only after you accept a request, and either person can close it whenever they want. Posts are only visible to logged-in users.

Nobody gets left on read.

  • Every post has a deadline. When it passes, open requests expire on their own, or the owner can close them early.
  • Declines are polite. Owners pick a kind reason and can add a note of their own.
  • Everyone is told. When a post expires, each requester gets a note, so you are never left guessing.
  • Second chances exist. Owners can revive an expired or declined request if plans change.
